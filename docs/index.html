About GWAM

A Tanzanian company built to solve real needs.

GWAM is based in Dar es Salaam and works across Tanzania. Our idea is simple: find out what people need, then solve it.

That is why we run four businesses under one name. Tailors get their machines and supplies. Farmers get tractors, drones and testing. Landowners and builders get accurate surveys. And anyone with a big job can hire the machine to do it.

We want to help Tanzania grow, one workshop, one farm and one project at a time.
