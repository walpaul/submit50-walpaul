## Rules for scripts
- Keep their phrases, but cut each spoken line short. Their natural sentences run long, so split them into beats.
- Keep the "my friend" warmth. No corporate jargon or hype words.
- Use real machines, crops and places. Never invent numbers, deals or customers.
